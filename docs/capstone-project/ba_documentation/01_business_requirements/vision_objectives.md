## 1. Business Vision & Objectives

### 1.1 Product Vision

For IT administrators, monitoring operators, and maintenance technicians who need to understand the operational state of a simulated data center quickly and act on it in context, the AR-Based Infrastructure Monitoring and Maintenance System is a centralized monitoring and maintenance platform that helps users observe infrastructure health, respond to alerts, and carry out field inspection in a more structured way.

Unlike manual workflows that require users to switch between separate dashboards, notes, and inspection steps, the system provides a single operational view that connects infrastructure status, alerts, incident handling, and maintenance inspection in one workflow.

The long-term vision of the product is to become a practical operations support platform for simulated infrastructure environments, where monitoring, incident handling, and maintenance activities are connected through a consistent operational context. The product aims to reduce fragmented information, support faster response to issues, and make maintenance tasks easier to carry out and verify.

### 1.2 Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

1. To centralize infrastructure monitoring information so users can view the health status of simulated assets, related alerts, and recent operational history in one place.
2. To support role-based operational workflows by separating responsibilities for administrators, monitoring operators, and maintenance technicians.
3. To reduce manual effort in issue handling by connecting alert review, incident creation, ticket assignment, and inspection follow-up into one workflow.
4. To improve maintenance accuracy by linking each inspected asset with the correct operational context and inspection record.
5. To provide a realistic proof-of-concept for context-aware maintenance support in a simulated data center environment.
6. To support basic operational reporting and traceability by preserving audit trails, inspection results, and workflow status changes.
